Plotery is a modern, intuitive, and universal story planner for games, movies, novels, etc.You can add plots, characters, locations, objects, and additional ideas.

For plots, you can specify its title, purpose (e.g. what will this plot reveal?), conflict (e.g. what challenge does the plot introduce?), and the plot itself.

For characters, you can specify their name, role (e.g. what is their purpose?), age, personality, appearance, desire (e.g. what do they want?), and additional descriptions.

For locations, you can specify its name, layout (e.g. what does this place look like?), mood (e.g. should this place feel and be described as scary, cozy, old?), and additional descriptions.

For objects, you can specify its name, purpose (e.g. what will this object be used for?), appearance, location, and additional descriptions.

For ideas, you can specify its title and content.

Stories are saved as Plotery files so you can come back to your story later.

Everything is responsive and fits nicely on many screen sizes.
